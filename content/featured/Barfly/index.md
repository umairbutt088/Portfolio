The main idea of Hidden Gems is to give people in the community information and reviews of the best restaurants near them, and to highlight deals offered by those restaurants and bars.

Users can write reviews about specials shared by other users or by restaurant owners. Restaurant owners can also create **last-call bags**: when there is surplus food at the end of service, they can offer it at a lower price so nothing goes to waste and the community benefits.
