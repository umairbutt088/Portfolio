Rehearsal Master is an application to memorize lines, perfect your delivery, and collaborate seamlessly—all in one AI-powered platform. It allows uploading sides from Actors Access, Casting Networks, or any other PDF. Assign and customize voices for each character, making teamwork more immersive. Rehearse with ease—play, pause, and record your script for perfect delivery.

The main goal of Rehearsal Master is to take your script rehearsals to the next level with the **Voice Library**, a powerful feature that lets you enhance your performances with a variety of voices.
