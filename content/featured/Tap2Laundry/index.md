Built and shipped a production marketplace connecting customers with verified local laundry, ironing and tailoring providers, supporting both pickup/delivery and drop-off flows.

End-to-end **customer** and **partner** experiences cover map-based discovery, booking, order tracking, in-app chat, ratings, and partner onboarding and verification. The **Supabase** backend (**PostgreSQL**, RLS, RPCs, Storage, Edge Functions) provides secure role-based access, while the same **Expo** codebase powers a responsive web marketplace for signed-out discovery. Releases shipped with **EAS** to TestFlight and Google Play, with English/Urdu localization.
